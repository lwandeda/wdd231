Gqeberha Fitness & Wellness Center

Individual Project Site Plan

1. Site Name

Gqeberha Fitness & Wellness Center

This name clearly identifies the purpose of the website and connects the fitness and wellness center to the Gqeberha community.

2. Site Purpose

The website provides residents of Gqeberha with information about fitness programs, wellness services, membership options, operating hours, and contact information. It will help visitors understand the services available and make it easier for them to choose a program and contact the center.

3. Scenarios

The following questions represent common information that visitors to the website may want to find.

  • What fitness programs are available for beginners?
  • How much does membership cost and what is included?
  • What are the center's operating hours?
  • What wellness services are available?

6. Wireframe

The wireframes show the planned layout of the home page for small and large viewports. They focus on structure, layout, and functionality before the final colors, images, and detailed design are added.

Small Viewport — Mobile

The mobile wireframe uses a vertical layout with a hamburger menu and stacked content sections.

Large Viewport — Desktop

The desktop wireframe uses the wider screen to display navigation and content sections with more horizontal space.
